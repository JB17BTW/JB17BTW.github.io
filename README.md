## Activities out of math
I organize online seminars for mathematicians called 'Ethics in mathematics'. Its goal is to analyse the relations between mathematics and power, and responsibilities of mathematics and science in the current crises humanity are facing. To do so, scientists, historians, economists and philosophers (and more I hope) will be invited to give their point of view on this topic. Among others, examples of specific topics of study will be the global warming, the support towards irresponsible companies and military services, and the development of AI.

I am also an active member of <a href="https://www.ahmath.org/">AHM</a>.
