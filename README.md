# Where I stand on AI
Experts diagnosed <a href="https://youtu.be/DDf25rNyT1A">Trump<a/> (and thus the system behind him) <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fascism_in_the_United_States#Donald_Trump_and_fascism">to be<a/> <a href="https://maisonfrancaise.columbia.edu/sites/maisonfrancaise.columbia.edu/files/content/Is%20It%20Fascism_%20A%20Leading%20Historian%20Changes%20His%20Mind.%20-%20The%20New%20York%20Times.pdf">a fascist<a/>. To get stronger, fascism usually <a href="https://youtu.be/DDf25rNyT1A?t=509">builds an alliance<a/> <a href="https://youtu.be/Grpw9BFx-E8?t=1">with the capital<a/>. <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/oct/05/trump-power-ai-techscape">Leaders<a/> of the <a href="https://stersoftware.com/news/how-major-ai-companies-are-becoming-entangled-with-the-trump-administration-2026-07-03-avond/">main AI companies<a/> showed themselves many times for being part of the <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/mar/08/billionaires-tech-oligarchs">US oligarchy<a/>. The term of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technofeudalism">techno-feudalism<a/> is often used by experts.

In democracies, universities have the role to develop and spread knowledge to fight power when this one lies to the public. Likewise, I believe mathematicians and scientists, have the moral obligation to work with the aim to benefit human kind and values, at least not making the human situation worst (but I could also say toward the world and its inhabitant). This is not the case when one promotes companies who are co-creating fascist regimes, who are known for <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/may/05/publishers-sue-meta-copyright-ai">stealing<a/> <a href="https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2026-07-23/judge-approves-anthropics-1-5-billion-settlement-with-authors">intellectual<a/> <a href="https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/08/u-s-textbook-authors-sue-openai-for-copyright-infringement/">property<a/>, <a href="https://www.cbc.ca/player/play/video/9.7311779">methodically<a/> destroy antic books and make their content private (do you hear the sound of obscurantism?), <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cvqi8UG5YlA">undermine our chances<a/> <a href="https://unu.edu/inweh/collection/environmental-cost-of-AIs-Enrgy-Use-Carbon-water-and-land-footprints">to limit the consequences of global warming<a/>, encourage excessive consumption of resources (which is the main reason for the extinction of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropocene#Nature_of_human_effects">biodiversity in number of species and number of individuals<a/>), develop weapons for military purposes and mass surveillance, destroy our spaces of creation and freedom. 

The good news is the following: AI companies base their economy on a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_bubble#Speculation">financial<a/> <a href="https://youtu.be/Yu-wqYCyInU?t=2669">bubble<a/>, very close to a <a href="https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2026-07-29/nvidias-750-billion-ai-bet-deepens-fears-of-circular-tech-bubble">circular tech bubble<a/>. It means that these companies need to keep our trust at all cost. So not only do we have a responsibility not to support companies who make fascism possible, but we also have the responsibility not to help them in making us public billboard for their business. Their survival partially depend on our current reaction. Mathematicians, academics in general, are regarded as part of the elites of their countries. A signal from them can make a difference in the behavior of the population. The AI companies perfectly understood that, and that is why they try to get scientists so badly. Don't fall in the trap my friend. For the ones who don't have the power and the privileges we have, we need to fight.
