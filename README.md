# Ethics in Mathematics
From this year, I organize a series of seminars whose goal is to analyse the relations between mathematics and power, and responsibilities of mathematics and science in the current crises humanity is facing. To do so, scientists, historians, economists and philosophers (and more I hope) will be invited to give their point of view on this topic. Among others, examples of specific topics of study will be the global warming, the support towards irresponsible companies and military services, and the development of AI.
